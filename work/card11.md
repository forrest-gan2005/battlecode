# [Idea] Queen fallback: circle near a portal in a kelp corner

**Idea by:** Zhe (Discord, Oct 3). Related: #2 (queen survival, QWQ), and the safe-zone idea above.

## Claim
If no enclosed safe zone has been found yet, the queen waits in a corner spot surrounded by kelp on three sides, ideally with a portal nearby. She circles within two tiles of that portal, so the portal works as an emergency escape route if it gets too dangerous.

## Evidence
- Zhe's observation and reasoning.
- The queen dies early in most of our ladder games (159/228, median round 123).

## Expected signal
Fewer early queen deaths (before round 150), and fewer queen rams in open space.

## What would show it's wrong
The queen gets boxed in at the corner, or dies when the escape portal leads into enemies.
