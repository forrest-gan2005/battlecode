# [Idea] Queen safe zone: enclosed area behind a portal, confirmed by sonar

**Idea by:** Zhe (Discord, Oct 3). Related: #2 (queen survival, QWQ). Please coordinate with QWQ before testing.

## Claim
1. When any dragon finds a portal leading to an area fully boxed in by kelp on all four sides, it broadcasts that portal's coordinates right after coming out.
2. When the queen gets the signal, she moves towards that portal.
3. Once close, she sends sonar towards the portal, aimed at the opposite side, for at least two rounds in a row. If the echoes come back as kelp, the area is safe and she goes in.
4. Inside, the queen's head must never end on the tiles right next to the portal.
5. She stays in the safe zone until the late game, for example when feeding starts. If the enemy queen is knocked out, our queen never leaves the zone.

## Evidence
- Zhe's observation and reasoning.
- Our queen died in 159 of 228 meow ladder games, median round 123 (overnight analysis).

## Expected signal
On maps that contain such an enclosed area: our queen alive at the end more often, and fewer queen deaths before round 300.

## What would show it's wrong
The queen dies on the way in, or near the portal; or the enemy reaches her inside.
